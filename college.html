College fragment

Status: in progress

College is so beautiful. Because for 4 short, infinite, precious, terrible years, you get to be a friend. Your friends will love and you will love infinitely hard and fast and it will burn up in the atmosphere before you can hold it.

Or maybe, just maybe, you will be lucky enough to hold the wax as it runs between your fingers. And you will borrow grief from the future, knowing that the wax will run more the tighter you try to hold it. So as you sit around the table with your friends, one last time, sharing and breaking bread of love, try not to feel as though you live already in a memory of a time long ago.

Then the wax will be gone. You will feel your love stretch across miles, and while it will not break you, it will make you unspeakably sad.

But, as they say, grief is just love. Leaving the body, but love all the same. And you will think to yourself, oh how lucky I am to have loved someone so hard to make the miles feel like grief. To feel sorrow so deep because it matches the depths of my love for my friends.

And though we are all on our own journeys, and we no longer hear about each day from each other, I remember the sound of laughter around a table, and I carry a small bit of sunlight from the past with me.
